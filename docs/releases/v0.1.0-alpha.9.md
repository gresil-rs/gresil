## Notes (the release body)

**`neuralos-snn` 0.1.0-alpha.9** — the `no_std`, i16 fixed-point
spiking-neuron library for RISC-V edge silicon. The pub walk: what
stops being public before 0.1.0 promises the surface.

Since alpha.8, the library's surface is smaller and each piece of it
says what it does: ten names at the root (eleven with `unstable-stdp`),
no public field on the neuron or the synapse, one error variant per
rule, the current named in pA, STDP and the closed bridge chapter
behind unstable features, and a network that does not learn unless
asked. NIR graphs import at true scale, a two-layer graph builds, and a
stranger's `.nir` reaches the ESP32-C3 in one command. The fourteen
checks of `docs/ROADMAP.md` § 0.1.0 hold.

**Breaking, by module.** One line per change since alpha.8, each naming
the commits whose BREAKING paragraphs it folds; every paragraph is one
command away:

```bash
git log --grep='^BREAKING' v0.1.0-alpha.8..v0.1.0-alpha.9
```

51 commits carry one at `ec17c31`, each with `!` in its subject.

*The crate root.*

- The root keeps ten of alpha.8's 59 names: `FixedNetwork`,
  `FixedSynapse`, `LIFNeuron`, `NeuronType`, `VoltageResolution`,
  `NirImportOptions`, `Synapse`; with `std`, `NetworkTopology` and
  `SpikingNeuralNetwork`; with `unstable-stdp`, `STDPRule`. It gains
  `NirImport`, with `std`. The other 49 leave the root in every build
  (`error[E0432]`): 44 keep their module path, `lif_neuron::`,
  `network::`, `nir::`, `spike_recorder::`, `synapse::`, `trit::` (the
  bridge's and the kernel's with `unstable-bridge`,
  `trit::stochastic_ternary_flip` with `unstable-stdp`), and five are
  gone, each in its module's line below. The module `stats` is private:
  write `network::NetworkStats` (`958c43b`, `74fb1b1`, `9796e03`,
  `5c9cadb`, `adbd0af`, `7f22101`).
- `Error` is `#[non_exhaustive]` and names each refusal's rule:
  `InvalidParameter` and `SpikeHistoryMissing` are gone and
  `IndexOutOfBounds` is `NeuronIdOutOfRange`, so a match on any of the
  three stops compiling; a refusal returns the variant of its rule, one
  of sixteen unit variants. A cast with `as` still compiles and reads
  numbers that are not promised (`ea618a6`, `ed78e2d`, `8ee5b44`; the
  numbers, `1f90765` and `a4ff426`).

*`lif_neuron`.*

- One constructor, `LIFNeuron::new(id, neuron_type, resolution)`, a
  `const fn`: a caller of alpha.8's `new(id)` writes the two it built,
  `NeuronType::Excitatory` and `VoltageResolution::Millivolt`.
  `new_with_type_resolution` is gone, its callers call `new`.
  `LIFNeuron`, `VoltageResolution` and `NeuronType` no longer implement
  `Default` (`3d31028`).
- `LIFNeuron`'s 17 public fields are crate-private. Fourteen have a
  getter, nine under the field's name and five renamed:
  `refractory_period_us()` (the field `tau_refractory_us`),
  `refractory_remaining_us()` (`refractory_time_us`), and the currents
  in pA, `synaptic_current_pa()`, `adaptation_current_pa()` and
  `noise_amplitude_pa()` (`synaptic_current_ua`,
  `adaptation_current_ua`, `noise_amplitude_ua`). The capacitance,
  `capacitance_pf`, which no computation read, is gone; the two stamps,
  `last_update_time_us` and `last_spike_time_us`, have no getter. The
  id, the type and the grid are set by `new` alone, the other
  parameters by their `with_*` setters, and the state by
  `integrate_and_fire`, `add_synaptic_current`,
  `clear_synaptic_current`, `decay_adaptation_current`, `reset` and the
  chain, whose `with_resting_potential` moves the membrane with the
  rest. The neuron is 40 bytes, where it was 44, on x86-64 and on
  riscv32imc (`24043cc`, `bdabd86`, `3d31028`, `a871884`).
- A neuron built by `new` is silent: its noise amplitude is 0, where
  alpha.8's constructors gave 5. A network from
  `SpikingNeuralNetwork::new` or `new_with_voltage_resolution` still
  gives its neurons 5 pA; a neuron of your own asks for it with
  `with_noise_amplitude_pa(5)` (`a6b34dc`).
- `NeuronType` is `#[non_exhaustive]` (`95fdffc`).

*`network` (with `std`).*

- Both constructors start with plasticity off, feature or not, where
  alpha.8 started it on: a default build does not learn (`21cae7c`).
- The networks' clock is a `u64`, the neuron's stamps stay 32 bits:
  `SpikingNeuralNetwork::current_time_us()` is `time_us()`, and it,
  `FixedNetwork::time_us()` and `Spike::time_us` return or hold a `u64`
  where they held a `u32`; a caller that kept one in a `u32` converts.
  The clock no longer stops at the `u32` ceiling, 2^32 − 1 µs, 71.6 min
  of model time (`8184910`, `420e91f`).
- `Spike` is `#[non_exhaustive]` and its `weight` is gone; it is 16
  bytes on x86-64, where it was 8 (`9587cf0`, `52a7eff`, `8184910`).
- `NetworkStats` is `#[non_exhaustive]`; `total_neurons` and
  `total_synapses` are gone, read `neuron_count()` and
  `synapse_count()`; its `Default`, which gave 0.0 where a network's
  stats start at −70.0, is gone (`9587cf0`).
- `NetworkTopology` is `#[non_exhaustive]` (`95fdffc`).
- `spike_history()` is gone: keep the `Vec<Spike>` that `step` returns
  (`20ae618`).
- The CSR is private: `SparseSynapseMatrix`, `SynapseIter`,
  `IncomingIter` and the module `csr` are gone from every path, and
  `finalize_synapses` is no longer public. Delete the call: a step that
  follows an add re-sorts the CSR, O(S + N) for S synapses and N
  neurons, once for a batch of adds; a caller who adds between steps
  pays it at each step after an add (`5c9cadb`, `daee0c5`, `1f90765`).
- `build_topology` refuses a `Feedforward` list whose first layer has
  no neuron, which built with no synapse from it, and a list past
  65,535 neurons with the layer rule's refusal, where release summed
  the sizes in a `u16` and returned `InvalidParameter`, or
  `IndexOutOfBounds` when the sum wrapped to the count; it refuses a
  `Random` connectivity or a `SmallWorld` rewiring probability outside
  0 to 1, NaN included, with `ProbabilityOutOfRange`, where it clamped,
  and NaN wired or rewired nothing (`2253054`, `69edb16`).
- `set_synaptic_input_divisor` refuses a divisor above 32,767 with
  `DivisorOutOfRange`: such a divisor gave sign-flipped pulses, and
  65,535 a panic on a weight of `i16::MIN` (`d437aaa`).
- `from_neurons` refuses more than 65,535 neurons
  (`NeuronCountOutOfRange`), which panicked on the first step or
  stamped wrong ids; neurons on more than one voltage grid
  (`MixedVoltageGrids`), which reported a wrong membrane average; and a
  neuron not at rest (`NeuronNotAtRest`) or whose id is not its
  position (`NeuronIdMismatch`) (`f89220c`, `7f914c9`, `3ba9055`).
- `new` and `from_neurons` refuse a neuron whose membrane cannot move at
  the time step, with `FrozenMembrane`: a membrane time constant of 0
  or above 1,000 steps. `new` builds its own neurons, so it refuses a
  step under 20 µs, or under 10 µs when every neuron is inhibitory
  (`a4ff426`).
- `reset` gives a fresh network's stats: `avg_membrane_potential_mv`
  −70.0 and `firing_rate_hz` 0.0, where it kept the last step's
  (`7bbfabc`; with `unstable-stdp`, the STDP group below).

**Frozen modules.** No published version has `fixed::freeze` or the
setters its modules call. alpha.8's package carried only its trace
tests' writer, whose `LIFNeuron { … }` literals of every field stop
compiling with the fields above: write such a module again with this
release's freezer.

**Why the current is in pA.** The step computes the current's move as
R·I/1000 in mV, and with the resistance in MΩ that holds only for a
current in pA, since pA·MΩ is µV. alpha.8's names and docs said µA, a
factor of 10^6 off the arithmetic, since the port. The rename moved no
value, only the names and the docs: a current of 160 is 160 pA.

**Behavior that changed, not a break.**

- `NetworkStats::avg_membrane_potential_mv` is the mean of all
  neurons, as its rustdoc always said; alpha.8 sampled every tenth
  neuron and scaled the sum by ten (`49df56f`).
- `NetworkStats::firing_rate_hz` follows the clock past 71.6 min of
  model time: alpha.8's clock stopped at the `u32` ceiling,
  2^32 − 1 µs, and the rate, total spikes over the clock's seconds,
  climbed without bound from there. The stall did more: the noise
  seed, `id ^ current_time_us`, froze; every spike carried one time;
  and STDP, on by default in alpha.8, paired each pre spike with any
  post that had fired since the ceiling as simultaneous, and
  potentiated it by 5 (`8184910`).
- A step delivers each synapse's pulse whatever order the synapses were
  added in, and `FixedNetwork::try_from` takes a network wired in any
  order: in alpha.8 edges added out of `pre` order with no
  finalize after them, an edge added after `build_topology`, or a
  second finalize made the step deliver pulses under other synapses'
  edges, and `try_from` could build a network that parted from the std
  one (`83d401a`, `1f90765`). `try_from` accepts a fresh network, which
  starts with plasticity off.
- Refused, where they panicked: `build_topology` on a `Feedforward`
  list with a zero layer after the first, or with sizes past 65,535 in
  a debug build, and on a `Balanced` ratio that leaves no excitatory
  neuron, the default topology on one neuron among them (`2253054`).
- `new` takes any topology and reserves nothing. alpha.8's sized its
  vectors from the topology before any rule ran, so a connectivity of
  1e30 or infinity panicked it, a topology too big to reserve for
  aborted the process, and in a debug build a `Balanced` ratio above 1
  could panic it. `build_topology` refuses that ratio with
  `MissingNeuronType`, a list the layer rule refuses with
  `BadLayerSizes` and that connectivity with `ProbabilityOutOfRange`;
  a network too big for the machine still dies there, as its synapses
  grow (`2253054`, `aa6e1d9`).
- `build_topology` on `SmallWorld` near 65,535 neurons no longer panics
  in a debug build, nor wires edges off its ring and parallel edges in
  release; on `Random` it stores no list of every pair, which aborted
  at 65,535 neurons, and wires as before (`140f34c`, `7a5d0d9`).
- The NIR import's memory grows with the weights, not with the square
  of a root's width: alpha.8 composed each stage from an `f64` identity
  as wide as its root on both sides (`d42920e`; ISA round 56 has the
  figures).
- A LIF record's `capacitance_pf` is the substrate's own τ over its own
  R, `round(tau_us / r_mohm)`, so a source whose τ or R is off its grid
  imports with the substrate's capacitance, and a re-import gives the
  same one; the source's stays in the record as `tau_s / r_ohm`
  (`e86bb93`).
- `quantize_lif` refuses a LIF whose τ is past 1,000 steps,
  `TauTooLongForDt`, where it built a neuron that never moved; at a
  0.1 ms step that is a β above 0.999, and snnTorch's own 0.999 is
  refused: in float32 its τ is 100,001 µs (`ddd7507`).
- The import's edge walk reads a node's field 64 containers deep, as the
  scan and the node walk do; a field 63 or 64 deep was refused as
  malformed JSON (`535958c`).
- `NirError::Json`'s message reads "JSON the reader cannot read at byte
  {p}: …", where it read "malformed JSON at byte {p}" (`874d83d`).

**The crate as shipped.** The default build forbids `unsafe` (only
`simd` has it) and has `missing_docs` on; the README's first example is
a doctest; the rustdoc states the neuron's semantics and the step's
order, the one-step delay among them. docs.rs marks each item with the
feature it needs; the attribute that does it is nightly's, so a stable
`cargo doc` with `--cfg docsrs` stops on E0554. The starter set, four
examples a stranger copies,
`stranger`, `freeze`, `board` and `learner`, ships in the package, each
with its test, and CI checks `board` and `learner` on riscv32imc; each
example names the features it needs in the manifest, `std` among them,
`unstable-stdp` for `demo` and `trace`, `unstable-freeze` for `trace`
and `freeze`; the four `ternary_*` examples are the repository's, not
the package's. The MSRV,
`rust-version` 1.92, is checked on 1.92.0 in six configurations: `std`,
`no_std`, `simd`, `unstable-stdp`, `unstable-bridge`,
`unstable-freeze`.

**On the ESP32-C3 (rv32imc, 160 MHz), the same SuperMini.** A
stranger's `.nir` reaches the board in one command,
`firmware/esp32c3/stranger.sh run <file.nir>`, which converts it,
checks its capacity and replays it on the chip bit for bit. Capacity
is a bar, `44·N + 6·S ≤ 262,144` bytes of arrays, measured at its two
corners on the board with a 44-byte neuron, so with today's 40 bytes
it is an upper bound. On the last board round of record (round 67,
`evidence/esp32c3-bringup/README.md` § Twelfth entry) the fourteen
plasticity-off traces, the two LIF→Linear→LIF witnesses among them,
replay on the chip bit for bit: `14 cases, 0 red`. The frozen
`feedforward-8` (8 neurons, 6 synapses) steps in 14,143 ns per step of
the whole network, 14,058 at alpha.8, with alpha.8's 3,377 spikes from
step 5 and its checksum. The two neuron arms build their neuron with
`new`, so they run without noise: 147 spikes from step 55 as in
alpha.8, the checksum `8cd1e63b` where alpha.8 read `0b78b456`. The
same fourteen traces replay bit for bit under QEMU `riscv64gc`.

**The converter**, `neuralos-nir2json`, the static musl binary in
§ Files, turns a stranger's `.nir` file (HDF5, read in pure Rust) into
the JSON the library imports. Since `bringup-2026-09-09`, the last
release that carried it: `--sim-units` reads the simulation-unit
exports of snnTorch, norse and rockpool at true scale, so its output
changes, `r`, the potentials, the sidecar's fields and the default step
(`5007028`); the JSON marks its units (`7d8581a`); `Affine` nodes
convert under it (`aba2f94`), and its neurons have no adaptation, as
NIR's LIF has none (`f25b26f`). `--freeze` writes the frozen module the
board runs (`455c92c`), driven by `--drive` runs (`2a35a12`). It
refuses by name, exit 2, where it converted: a dataset or group its
group does not carry (`cbbe4a9`), an Input's or an Output's empty shape
(`b818520`), a file past `MAX_VALUES` values or with a chunk past it
(`2dbbec0`), and under `--freeze` a Linear weight of ±f64's largest
(`a4069d5`). Its known limits, the next converter PR's: a
variable-length string's payload is not in the size count; a soft or
external link can pass unseen; two datasets, the file's `version` and
the graph's `node/type`, are read before the filter check; and of the
exports it reads, only snnTorch's are checked against values from
outside, the parity test against snnTorch's own spikes.

**The road to 0.1.0** is `docs/ROADMAP.md` § 0.1.0: fourteen checks a
stranger can run, all fourteen holding at this release.
